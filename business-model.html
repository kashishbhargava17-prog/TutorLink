DIGITAL ECONOMY

How TutorLink creates, delivers and captures value.

TutorLink connects students who need personalized learning with tutors who want to earn through their knowledge. The platform coordinates the exchange, manages the digital experience, and earns through a commission on completed tutoring transactions.

CORE MODEL Students ↔ TutorLink ↔ Tutors A two-sided digital marketplace

REVENUE MODEL

Where the money flows

TutorLink's primary revenue comes from a 12% commission on tutoring transactions.

STUDENT PAYMENT ₹300 Example 60-minute session
→
TUTORLINK ₹36 12% commission
→
TUTOR ₹264 88% of student payment
🌱
Access Fund allocation

5% of TutorLink's ₹36 commission = ₹1.80 allocated toward the TutorLink Access Fund.

Important: TutorLink's commission is platform revenue, not profit. Operating costs such as technology, support, marketing, payment processing, and other expenses must be deducted before calculating profit or loss.
