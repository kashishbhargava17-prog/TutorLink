COMMON QUESTIONS

Frequently asked questions

How do I book a tutor?

Go to Find Tutors, choose a tutor, open their profile, select a tutoring package, choose your preferred day and time, and complete the demo booking flow.

What are Classes Remaining?

Classes Remaining shows how many sessions from your active monthly package have not yet been used. For example, 8 / 12 means eight classes remain from a twelve-class package.

What is Tute?

Tute is TutorLink's learning and loyalty reward. It is not money and it does not represent academic marks. Students can earn Tute through activities such as completing quizzes, tutoring sessions, and submitting session feedback.

How does the rating reward work?

After a completed tutoring session, students can submit a 1–5 star rating. Any honest rating earns 10 Tute. The reward is connected to that specific completed session, so submitting the same rating again does not create another reward.

What happens if my tutor cancels?

TutorLink can offer a rescheduled class, a replacement tutor, or a refund depending on the situation. If a tutor does not show up, the class can be restored and additional Tute compensation may apply.

Can I cancel a class?

Cancellations made 12 or more hours before a session can normally be rescheduled or qualify for an eligible refund. Cancellations made less than two hours before a class will normally count as used, subject to genuine emergency exceptions.

Are TutorLink payments real?

This version of TutorLink is a prototype. Payment screens are demonstration flows and do not process real transactions. A production platform would use a secure payment provider.

Is the virtual classroom real?

The classroom is a working prototype interface demonstrating the intended learning workflow. A production version would integrate a real-time video-conferencing service.
