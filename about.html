OUR MISSION

Build a learning ecosystem where knowledge creates value.

Traditional tutoring often depends on personal connections, local availability, and fragmented communication. TutorLink turns that experience into a connected digital ecosystem.

Students can discover tutors, compare learning options, schedule sessions, track progress, earn Tute, and build a continuous learning journey.

Tutors can use the same ecosystem to showcase their expertise, find students, manage availability, teach online, receive feedback, and build their teaching journey.

✦
THE TUTORLINK PHILOSOPHY

Earn through learning.
Reinvest in learning.

TutorLink is designed to create value for both sides of the marketplace while directing part of the platform's commission toward greater access to learning.

TUTORLINK ACCESS FUND

Every transaction can help create another learning opportunity.

TutorLink allocates 5% of its commission toward the Access Fund. The fund is designed to support subsidized learning opportunities for eligible students.

TRUST & SAFETY

A learning marketplace needs trust.

TutorLink is designed around transparent interactions, honest feedback, and responsible platform practices.

✓
Tutor Verification

Tutor profiles can move through a verification process before receiving verified status.

⭐
Honest Feedback

Students can rate completed sessions from 1–5 stars. Rewards are for submitting feedback, not for giving a positive rating.

🔐
Data Protection

A production platform would use secure authentication, protected databases, and appropriate privacy controls.

🛟
Human Support

Routine questions can use the assistant, while complex payment, privacy, refund, and safety matters can be escalated to human support.

Prototype transparency

TutorLink is a college digital economy project prototype. Tutor profiles, transactions, rewards, verification, virtual classrooms, and other platform features shown in this website are demonstrations of the proposed ecosystem. A production platform would require secure backend systems, real payment processing, real-time communication infrastructure, privacy controls, and formal tutor verification.
