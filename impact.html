TUTORLINK ACCESS FUND

Earn through learning. Reinvest in learning.

TutorLink allocates 5% of its platform commission toward subsidized learning opportunities for eligible students.

Example

If a student pays ₹300 for a session, TutorLink's 12% commission is ₹36. The Access Fund allocation is 5% of that commission — ₹1.80.

The Access Fund allocation is calculated from TutorLink's commission, not from the student's total payment.

CA-2 DIGITAL ECONOMY SIMULATION

Impact across changing business conditions

TutorLink's digital economy is designed to respond to changes in users, competition, trust, automation, and scale.

01 Network Effect

More students can create demand for tutors, while tutor participation expands the range of learning options available.

02 Competition

TutorLink can focus on differentiated value such as personalized matching, Tute, and its Access Fund.

03 Trust

Ratings, verification workflows, support, and responsible data practices help strengthen the marketplace relationship.

04 AI Opportunity

AI-assisted routine support can reduce repetitive work while human support remains available for complex or sensitive cases.

05 Green Growth

Efficient digital infrastructure and resource monitoring can support growth while considering environmental and operating impact.
